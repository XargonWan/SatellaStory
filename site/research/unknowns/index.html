    Unknowns & Research Gaps¶

    Introduction¶

    This page documents what we do not know about BS-X and the Satellaview. These are the open questions, missing pieces, and leads that need to be pursued. Every entry here represents an opportunity for discovery through interviews, archival research, or community outreach.

    The BS-X Story & Narrative¶

    The Unfinished Story¶

    Question Details Priority
    What was the planned ending? Yusuke Akamatsu stated the story was never given a proper ending and was rebooted mid-service. What was the original planned conclusion?1 ★★★
    What was the "2010" side story? Akamatsu serialized a story called "2010" in Satellaview-Tsushin magazine. What did it contain? Where can copies be found?1 ★★★
    How many chapters/episodes were planned? The town's story evolved over the service's lifetime. Was there a specific episode count planned? ★★
    Was there a complete narrative bible? Did Nintendo/St.GIGA create a design document or story bible for the town and its characters? ★★★

    Characters¶

    Question Details Priority
    Who were all the NPCs? We know a handful of NPCs (fortune teller, postman, old man at download center) but not all. A complete NPC roster with names and dialogue is missing. ★★
    What did each NPC say? The full dialogue tree of every NPC in the town has not been fully extracted and documented. ★★
    Were there time-limited NPCs? Some characters may have appeared only during specific broadcasts or seasons. Which ones, and when? ★★
    What was the "mad professor" storyline? Akamatsu mentioned "TV-headed children enchanted by a mad professor" -- what was this subplot?1 ★★

    Locations¶

    Question Details Priority
    What is the complete town map? The Satellaview History Museum had a map page, but it was not fully archived. How many screens/locations exist?2 ★★
    Were there secret areas? Were there hidden or unlockable areas in the town that required specific in-game conditions? ★★
    Did the town change over time? The Satellaview could update the town via broadcast. What changed between 1995 and 1999? ★★★

    Items & Currency¶

    Question Details Priority
    What was the full item list? Players could buy telephone cards, vehicle tickets, fish bait, shoes for dashing. What else existed?3 ★
    How did the economy work? How did players earn in-game currency? What was the pricing of items? ★
    Were there rare/event-only items? Were some items only available during specific broadcasts? ★★

    Technical Unknowns¶

    Broadcast Infrastructure¶

    Question Details Priority
    How was the St.GIGA broadcast center set up? What equipment did St.GIGA use to encode and transmit game data? ★★
    Where was the St.GIGA studio located? The physical location of the broadcast center is not well documented. ★
    How many staff operated the Satellaview service? The size of the Satellaview operations team at St.GIGA is unknown. ★★
    What happened to the broadcast servers? After St.GIGA dissolved, what happened to the physical broadcast equipment and data servers? ★★★

    BS-X Cartridge¶

    Question Details Priority
    Are there different BS-X ROM revisions? Multiple BS-X cartridge revisions may exist. How many, and what changed? ★★
    What unused content exists? TCRF documented unused music and text, but the full extent is unknown.4 ★★
    How did the online leaderboard system work? BS-X supported score submission. What was the technical implementation? ★★
    Was there anti-piracy/region locking? The BS-X cartridge had DRM that LuigiBlood patched. How did it work?5 ★

    Memory Pack¶

    Question Details Priority
    What is the complete list of Memory Pack commercial releases? Nintendo sold pre-loaded Memory Packs. A complete catalog is missing. ★★
    How many Memory Packs survive? No census of surviving Memory Packs exists. ★
    What data is still undumped? Many Memory Packs likely contain data that has never been dumped.6 ★★★

    Lost Broadcasts & Content¶

    SoundLink Games¶

    Question Details Priority
    How many SoundLink broadcasts are completely lost? Most SoundLink games were never recorded. An estimate of losses is needed. ★★★
    Were there any "lost" major titles? Are there major franchise games that were broadcast but never documented? ★★★
    What was the full schedule of every broadcast? The complete broadcast schedule across 1995-1999 is not fully reconstructed.2 ★★

    Magazines & Kabe Shinbun¶

    Question Details Priority
    How many magazine issues were broadcast? The full catalog of Satellaview magazine content is unknown. ★★
    What did the "kabe shinbun" (wall newspaper) contain? Only fragments have been recovered from recent dumps.6 ★★
    Are there Satellaview-Tsushin back issues? The Satellaview-Tsushin magazine contained developer interviews and side stories. Where are surviving copies? ★★★

    Audio Content¶

    Question Details Priority
    How much St.GIGA radio audio survives? VHS recordings captured some audio, but the total surviving archive is unknown. ★★★
    Were all SoundLink audio tracks lost? The radio narration and music for most SoundLink games exists only in fan recordings.7 ★★★
    What music was exclusive to broadcasts? Some music for BS Zelda, BS Fire Emblem etc. was never released on albums. How much is missing?8 ★★

    People We Cannot Find¶

    The ROMar Mystery¶

    Question Details Priority
    Who was ROMar? The single most important anonymous figure in Satellaview preservation. The source of the first ROM leaks. Never identified.9 ★★★
    How did ROMar obtain the ROMs? Were they a St.GIGA employee, a Nintendo contractor, or a collector with insider access? ★★★
    What else did ROMar have? Pachuka only leaked a portion of ROMar's collection. What other unreleased material existed? ★★★
    Is ROMar still alive? ROMar's current status is completely unknown. ★★★

    R&D2 Developers (Unknown Status)¶

    Of the ~35 developers in the BS-X hidden credits, only Noriko Kitamura has a confirmed current status (still at Nintendo). The whereabouts of the remaining ~34 developers are unknown. Many would be in their 50s-60s now.

    Priority targets for location: - Kazuhiko Taniguchi -- Succeeded Uemura as R&D2 GM. Most likely to have comprehensive knowledge. - Motomu Rikiishi -- Programmer (BS-X + Jupiter Corp). Dual-company perspective. - Masato Furukawa -- Programmer (BS-X + Jupiter Corp). Dual-company perspective. - Katsuya Nakagawa -- "Excavator" Nakagawa. Listed in BS-X credits.

    Business & Corporate Unknowns¶

    Question Details Priority
    What was Nintendo's total investment? The financial scale of the Nintendo-St.GIGA partnership is unknown. ★★
    How many Satellaview units were sold? Estimates vary. St.GIGA claimed 116,378 subscribers at peak in March 1997.10 But units sold vs. active subscribers is unclear. ★★
    What was the internal reaction at Nintendo to its failure? No internal Nintendo post-mortem has ever been made public. ★★★
    Was there a successor planned? The SatellaOFF group discussed "BS-X 2" rumors. Was there actually a successor in development?11 ★★★
    Why did Nintendo never release Satellaview content on other platforms? Most Satellaview games were never re-released. What were the legal/technical barriers? ★★

    Preservation Gaps¶

    Gap Details Priority
    No complete ROM set Not all Satellaview ROMs have been dumped. Some exist only on undumped Memory Packs. ★★★
    No complete broadcast schedule A day-by-day schedule of all Satellaview broadcasts 1995-1999 does not exist in any known source. ★★★
    No complete item/location database No one has fully catalogued every item, NPC, and location in BS-X. ★★
    No developer interview archive Only one Akamatsu interview and one Miyamoto interview from the era are known. ★★★
    No St.GIGA corporate archive When St.GIGA dissolved, corporate records were reportedly not preserved. ★★★
    No Nintendo internal documents Nintendo has never released internal documents about the Satellaview. ★★★

    The St.GIGA Bankruptcy: Missing Details¶

    Question Details Priority
    What exactly happened to St.GIGA? The timeline of St.GIGA's dissolution is unclear in multiple sources. Different dates are given for when the company ceased operations.12 ★★
    What was WINJ's role? World Independent Networks Japan took over St.GIGA's assets. What did they do, and why did they also fail?12 ★★
    Were St.GIGA's broadcast tapes preserved? St.GIGA likely had master tapes of all broadcasts. Were they archived or destroyed? ★★★
