    Fan Project Leads¶

    Con (BS Zelda Shrine)¶

    Field Details
    Handle Con, Conn
    Role BS Zelda Shrine founder (November 2, 1999), primary hacker, restoration patch developer
    Contact con.s@gmx.de
    Status Retired from active development (2016); minor updates through 2020

    Contributions¶

    • Founded the BS Zelda Shrine, one of the oldest English Satellaview sites1
    • Developed restoration patches for BS Zelda no Densetsu and Ancient Stone Tablets
    • Created the "Master Quest" hard mode version of AST
    • MSU-1 audio enhancement patches
    • Reconstructed bosses (Lanmola, Gleeok)
    • Coordinated the AST Master Quest event (April 2009)2

    Final Statement (2016)¶

    "The mission this site began in 1999 is with this release accomplished!"3

    Madick (Satellaview Heaven / Japanese Wiki)¶

    Field Details
    Handle Madick
    Role Japanese Satellaview blogger and wiki creator
    Contact Via atwiki.jp/bsxx admin system
    Status Inactive (last update 2016)

    Contributions¶

    • Maintained Satellaview Heaven blog (2007-2016), covering games, St.GIGA bankruptcy, and BS-X items5
    • Created the Japanese Satellaview Wiki at atwiki.jp/bsxx, the most comprehensive Japanese-language structured wiki6
    • Noted that the English Wikipedia Satellaview page referred to his blog as a reference5

    Kameb (Satellaview History Museum)¶

    Field Details
    Handle Kameb
    Role One of the first Satellaview documentarians
    Contact No known contact
    Status Inactive (last update 2008)

    Contributions¶

    • Created the Satellaview History Museum (~2000), one of the oldest Satellaview fan sites7
    • Documented the "Map of the Town Whose Name Was Stolen"
    • Compiled "Super Famicom Hour Program Schedule" (updated as late as February 2008)
    • Maintained SoundLink Game List7

    randnetdd (SFC Mania)¶

    Field Details
    Handle randnetdd
    Role Documented rare Satellaview games with original screenshots
    Contact randnetdd@mail.goo.ne.jp (likely dead)
    Status Inactive (last update November 2010)

    Notable Discoveries¶

    • Proved Jupiter Corporation's official Tamori no Picross broadcast dates were incorrect8
    • Documented 4 different title screens for Tamori no Picross
    • Discovered and documented "TestData 10/24" ROM naming mystery
    • Documented Magical Drop 2 JOQR Special Version8
