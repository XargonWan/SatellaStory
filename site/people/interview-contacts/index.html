    Interview Strategy: Original Developers¶

    Priority Matrix¶

    This page provides a strategic framework for reaching the original BS-X development team members. Unlike the fan community, these individuals are harder to reach but possess the primary knowledge about the system's creation, design intent, and unreleased content.

    Tier 1: Reachable (High Probability)¶

    1. Noriko Kitamura (北村 典子) -- Nintendo Employee¶

    Detail Information
    Role BS-X Designer, Satebô/Parabô mascot creator
    Current Still employed at Nintendo (1995-present)1
    Recent Work Nintendo World Championship (2024) - Planner
    Best Contact Method Via Nintendo corporate channels OR professional networking

    Strategy: - Contact Nintendo's PR department with a formal interview request - Reference her work on BS-X, SatellaWalker, and the Satellaview mascots - She was featured in an Iwata Asks interview (Jam with the Band) -- demonstrate knowledge of her career - Kyoto Report found her Facebook profile -- a respectful message there may work - Approach as a professional historian, not a fan

    Language: Japanese (primary) / may have some English proficiency Risk: Low -- she's a current Nintendo employee, so corporate policies may restrict what she can discuss Alternative: Contact via Nintendo's PR/Communications department

    2. Stephen Meyerink -- Translator/Intermediary¶

    Detail Information
    Role Japanese-to-English translator who worked on Satellaview-Tsushin interviews
    Contact sjmtaelus.com (contact form available)2
    Status Active freelance translator

    Strategy: - He already has context on BS-X history (translated the Akamatsu and Miyamoto interviews) - Could help locate Yusuke Akamatsu or facilitate contact - Could translate additional Japanese source materials - Professional relationship already established with Time Extension

    Best for: Locating Akamatsu, translating materials, introductions

    3. Jack Yarwood -- Time Extension Journalist¶

    Strategy: - Wrote the Satellaview feature for Time Extension (2023-2024) - Already interviewed Miyamoto and Akamatsu material - May have contact information for Akamatsu or leads on other sources - Professional journalist -- could collaborate on further research

    4. LuigiBlood (via Twitter/GitHub)¶

    • Already well-documented in the Researchers page
    • Has contact with the Japanese preservation community
    • May know how to reach former St.GIGA employees or Japanese developers

    Tier 2: Unknown Status (Requires Investigation)¶

    5. Yusuke Akamatsu (赤松 裕介) -- BS-X Writer¶

    Detail Information
    Role BS-X writer, "Super Famicom Happy Hour" producer
    Last Known 1995 Satellaview-Tsushin interview; fiction writer in the 1990s
    Status Unknown -- needs to be located

    Search Strategy: - He wrote novels under the name Yusuke Akamatsu -- search Japanese book databases (Amazon Japan, Kinokuniya) - Check if he published under "赤松裕介" (note: the Satellaview broadcast list mentions "Akamatsu Yuusuke Theater") - Stephen Meyerink may have leads from the translation work - Japanese publisher contacts for his books ("Scratch of Sun", "I, LOVE YOU, I, MISS YOU") - Search Japanese social media (Twitter Japan, mixi, Facebook)

    If found, key questions: - What was the planned ending for BS-X's story? - Was there a BS-X 2 planned? - What happened to the "2010" serialized story? - How did the collaboration with Nintendo R&D2 work? - Are there any story documents or design bibles that survive?

    6. Kazuhiko Taniguchi (谷口 和彦) -- Former R&D2 Manager¶

    Detail Information
    Role Succeeded Uemura as R&D2 GM in 2004; "Old Man Rider" in BS-X credits
    Status Unknown -- retired from Nintendo?

    Search Strategy: - Check if he stayed at Nintendo after the R&D2/SPD merger - Search Japanese professional networks (LinkedIn Japan, Wantedly) - Look for conference appearances or academic involvement (following Uemura's path) - May appear in Japanese game industry alumni networks

    7. Other BS-X Developers (R&D2 Staff)¶

    The hidden credits list ~30+ Nintendo R&D2 developers. Most of these people would now be in their 50s-60s. Many likely retired or moved to other companies. The Kyoto Report (kyoto-report.wikidot.com) has the most comprehensive database of Nintendo personnel and may have updated status for some.3

    Search strategy for each: 1. Search the name + "Nintendo" + "任天堂" on LinkedIn Japan 2. Search for Japanese game conference presentations 3. Check if they appear in other game credits (MobyGames, Kyoto Report) 4. Some may be on Twitter or Facebook

    8. St.GIGA Production Staff¶

    • Yakushiji Fumika (Production) -- search for current status
    • Hiroyuki Itakura (Voice Director) -- may be in voice acting industry
    • Akatsuki Takahashi (Promotion) -- may be in marketing/PR

    9. Jupiter Corporation Developers¶

    Jupiter Corp is still active (develops Picross games for Nintendo Switch). Contacting them via their corporate website may yield information about: - Motomu Rikiishi (Programmer, also in BS-X credits) - Masato Furukawa (Programmer, also in BS-X credits) - Corporate memory of the Satellaview development partnership

    Corporate contact: www.jupiter.co.jp (English site available)

    Tier 3: Public Figures (Accessible via Agents)¶

    10. Tamori (Kazuyoshi Morita)¶

    Detail Information
    Role Hosted Tamori no Picross; major Japanese TV personality
    Status Alive (born 1945), retired from "Waratte Iitomo!" in 2014
    Contact Via his talent agency

    Note: Tamori is a massive celebrity in Japan. Reaching him directly is nearly impossible, but his agency could be contacted. His involvement with Satellaview was relatively minor (a branded Picross game). Low priority.

    11. Ayumi Hamasaki¶

    • One of Japan's biggest pop stars
    • Her Satellaview appearances were very early in her career
    • Contact via her management agency (Avex)
    • Very low probability of response
