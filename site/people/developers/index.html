    Developers & Staff¶

    BS-X Development Team (Nintendo R&D2)¶

    The BS-X cartridge was developed by Nintendo Research & Development 2 Division (R&D2), led by general manager Masayuki Uemura.1 R&D2 was responsible for the Satellaview hardware and the BS-X software.

    Hidden Developer Credits¶

    BS-X contains a hidden staff roll accessible by entering "すたっふ", "スタッフ", "STAFF", or "staff" as the player name during initial setup. Each time the player confirms, a new developer nickname and name is displayed.2

    BS-X Writer¶

    Yusuke Akamatsu (赤松 裕介)¶

    Field Details
    Role Scenario writer for BS-X, producer for "Super Famicom Happy Hour" radio program
    Status Unknown -- last known activity was writing novels in the 1990s
    Notable Wrote the BS-X story, created the town's narrative, characters, and dialogue
    Influences Cited Twin Peaks (David Lynch) as a major inspiration for BS-X's tone8
    Other works Novels: "Scratch of Sun," "I, LOVE YOU, I, MISS YOU"; serialized side story "2010" in Satellaview-Tsushin magazine9
    Interview Gave a rare interview to Satellaview-Tsushin in May 1995, recently translated into English by Stephen Meyerink for Time Extension (January 2024)9

    Key quotes from the 1995 interview: - "Unlike a normal RPG, it has no combat, though it does include most of the other elements you'd expect from one." - "Right now there's only one background music track, and you can only go to the city, but after the summer we should be able to change the music, show photos via magazines, and develop it into something closer to a full-fledged RPG." - "I'd love to see fans get so into the experience that they do cosplay or make doujinshi. I'd certainly have no complaints if it got turned into a live-action show and became a much-discussed work like Twin Peaks."9

    Nintendo R&D1 (BS Tantei Club)¶

    Nintendo R&D1 developed BS Tantei Club: Yuki ni Kieru Kako. Key personnel:10

    Name Role
    Yoshio Sakamoto Director/Producer

    These three comprised "Team Shikamaru," responsible for scripts and scenarios for R&D1 titles including Detective Club, Super Metroid, and Card Hero.10

    ROMar (Unidentified)¶

    The single most important unknown figure in Satellaview preservation. Believed to have been a Satellaview ROM hoarder/collector in the late 1990s whose collection was leaked by Pachuka. His identity was never discovered by the community, despite extensive investigation by KiddoCabbusses.12

    Possibilities (unconfirmed speculation): - Former St.GIGA employee with access to broadcast masters - Nintendo contractor or developer - Game collector with insider connections - The debug features in the BS Dragon Quest ROM suggest a development environment connection13
