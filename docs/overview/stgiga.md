# St.GIGA

## Overview

**St.GIGA** (セント・ギガ) was a Japanese satellite radio broadcaster that partnered with Nintendo to provide data broadcasting services for the Satellaview. The company was founded in 1991 and began satellite radio broadcasts in 1993 on the BS-5 channel (NHK's satellite channel).[^1]

## Partnership with Nintendo

St.GIGA's partnership with Nintendo resulted from Nintendo's interest in satellite-based game distribution. Nintendo invested in St.GIGA, and the collaboration produced the Satellaview system, launched in 1995. Under the partnership, St.GIGA provided:[^2]

- **Satellite broadcast infrastructure** -- signal transmission, scheduling, and data encoding
- **Radio programming** -- live personalities, music, and narration for SoundLink games
- **Subscription management** -- user billing for the data broadcasting service
- **Studio facilities** -- for producing live radio segments synchronized with gameplay

## Broadcast Content

St.GIGA's "Super Famicom Hour" was the daily broadcast window (4:00 PM - 7:00 PM) dedicated to Satellaview content. The programming included three pillars:[^3]

1. **Radio programs** featuring celebrities including **Tamori** (talent/show host), **Ayumi Hamasaki** (singer), and comedy duo **Bakusho Mondai**
2. **Downloadable original games and add-on data**
3. **Sound Link games** -- the signature Satellaview format combining radio broadcasts with interactive gameplay

## Financial Difficulties

The Satellaview's business model proved unsustainable. Key factors:[^4]

- Inability to attract sufficient advertisers and sponsors
- Specialized nature of content limiting audience size
- Japan's economic downturn in the late 1990s
- High infrastructure costs for satellite broadcasting

Nintendo eventually withdrew from the partnership, leaving St.GIGA to continue solo broadcasts.

## Post-Satellaview Era

After the Satellaview service ended (~June 1999), St.GIGA continued operating as a satellite radio broadcaster. However, financial difficulties persisted:[^5]

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2000 | St.GIGA dissolves? (sources differ on exact date of dissolution) |
| ~2003 | Business transferred to **World Independent Networks Japan (WINJ)** |
| March 31, 2005 | End of BS analog broadcasting |
| ~2006 | WINJ broadcasts cease |
| November 2007 | WINJ declared legally "bankrupt" (破綻) |

The domain stgiga.jp was maintained by fans as a memorial site (St.GIGA Forever) until the early 2020s, when it was redirected to unrelated content.[^6]

## Personalities

St.GIGA employed several notable radio personalities who appeared during Satellaview broadcasts:
- **Tamori** (also known for hosting "Music Station" and "Waratte Iitomo!") -- hosted Tamori no Picross[^7]
- **Ayumi Hamasaki** -- early in her career, appeared in Satellaview programming
- **Bakusho Mondai** (comedy duo) -- hosted game-related segments

## Legacy

St.GIGA's legacy is intrinsically tied to the Satellaview. The company's pioneering work in satellite data broadcasting for video games was ahead of its time but commercially unsuccessful. The dissolution of St.GIGA contributed significantly to the loss of Satellaview archival materials, as corporate records, broadcast masters, and internal documentation were reportedly not preserved.[^8]
