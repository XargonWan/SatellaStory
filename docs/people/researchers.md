# Researchers & Archivists

## Matthew Callis

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Handles** | Matthew Callis, superfamicom |
| **Location** | Seattle, WA, USA |
| **Primary Role** | ROM dumper, preservationist, SNES development wiki maintainer |
| **Status** | Active (as of 2026) |

### Contributions

- Founded **Elude Visibility** (`eludevisibility.org`), a site dedicated to BS-X, prototype, unreleased, and unique ROM dumps[^1]
- Dumped **22 BS-X SRAM dumps** in 2011, the first successful dumps of battery-backed save data after years of being stuck[^2]
- Preserved **Kirby's Toy Box** by raising funds (with Frank Cifaldi) to win 4 cartridges at auction (¥85,500 / ~$813) and dumping: Circular Ball, Cannon Ball, Pachinko, Arrange Ball. Covered by Ars Technica and Nintendo Life[^3]
- Dumped the **Chaos Seed Satellaview demo** (June 2024) -- the first-ever dump of this demo streamed March 11, 1996[^4]
- Dumped **Star Soldier 2-Minute Version** from a Satellaview cart with partially-overwritten BS Zelda data; worked with ROM hackers to repair checksum[^5]
- Maintains **wiki.superfamicom.org**, the Super Famicom development wiki[^6]
- Ran **superfamicom.org/blog** (Satellablog) alongside KiddoCabbusses[^7]
- Contributed magazine scans (BS Zelda, BS Excitebike, BS Marvelous) to Satellablog[^8]
- Dumped and documented the Nintendo **"Game Kiosk" (GK Writer/Terminal)** official sales documents[^9]

### Known Satellaview Dumps

- BS Fuurai no Shiren - Surara wo Sukue - Dai-2
- BS Kirby no Omochabako - Pinball (with "SFC LiveProject Version2.00" string documentation)
- BS Konae Chan no Dokidoki Pengin Kazoku (limited number of startups noted)
- BS The Firemen[^10]

## LuigiBlood (Seru-kun / KiiroBomber)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Handles** | LuigiBlood (primary), formerly Seru-kun, KiiroBomber, Yakumono |
| **Location** | France |
| **Primary Role** | BS-X Project lead, Satellaview emulation, translation patch author |
| **Status** | Active (as of 2026) |

### Contributions

- Founded the **BS-X Project** (originally "Broadcast Satellaview") in September 2010[^15]
- Created **BS-X English Translation Patch** (v1.3 final, 2016) -- translates the entire BS-X town interface[^16]
- Developed **SatellaWave** (C#, MIT) -- Satellaview Server Manager for creating broadcast data binary files[^17]
- Worked with Revenant on **bsnes-plus Satellaview emulation**, including proper BS-X mapper and Memory Pack support[^18]
- Created **snes9x-sx2** fork, later upstreamed Satellaview support into official SNES9X (v1.55+)[^19]
- Helped ikari_01 implement Satellaview emulation on **sd2snes / FX Pak Pro** real hardware flashcarts[^20]
- Created first **Satellaview homebrew** application ("Hello Satellaview!", July 2010)[^21]
- **Memory Pack patches** for RPG Tsukuru 2 and Sound Novel Tsukuru (October 2010)[^22]
- Authored Satellaview technical documentation on the Super Famicom development wiki[^23]
- Also created the first **64DD disk dumper** and contributed 64DD emulation to MAME, CEN64, and Project64[^24]

## Matthias Nagler (d4s)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Handles** | d4s, Dieter Von Laser, Goldlocke, DocSchoko |
| **Location** | Germany |
| **Primary Role** | BS-X signal simulation pioneer, BS Excitebike patch, SNES homebrew |
| **Status** | Semiretired (as of 2021) |

### Contributions

- **First to attempt BS-X signal simulation** -- documented the Satellaview base modem register map and satellite stream protocol[^29]
- **BS Excitebike patch (2006)** -- patched Satellaview SoundLink Excitebike to behave like a normal SNES game[^30]
- Created **Super Road Blaster** (2011-2012) -- first MSU-1 SNES game, port of laserdisc game Road Blaster[^31]
- Created **Mario Kart R** -- complete Super Mario Kart ROM hack (2004/2020)[^32]
- Numerous other SNES homebrew projects and pirate cart cracking[^33]
- Cited as a "16-bit expert" by KiddoCabbusses on Satellablog[^34]

- **Website**: `dforce3000.de`[^36]

## KiddoCabbusses (Kiddo)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Handles** | KiddoCabbusses, Kiddo, Cabbusses |
| **Location** | Doylestown, Pennsylvania, USA |
| **Primary Role** | Satellablog founder, historian, community hub maintainer |
| **Status** | Active on Sonic Retro forums (as of Nov 2024) |

### Contributions

- Founded the original **Satellablog** at `satellablog.blogspot.com` (2008-2009)[^39]
- Relocated Satellablog to `superfamicom.org/blog` (2009)[^40]
- Founded **The English Satellaview Wiki** on Wikia/Fandom (September 2009)[^41]
- Created the most comprehensive English-language archive of Satellaview history and lore[^42]
- Documented the history of Satellaview emulation and ROM dumping[^43]
- Exposed eBay price-gouging of restored Satellaview content[^44]
- Maintained IRC presence (`#serioushax` on Caffienet) for community coordination[^45]
- Collaborated with LuigiBlood, Matthew Callis, and d4s on preservation efforts[^46]
- Accepted donations to fund further Satellaview material acquisition[^47]
