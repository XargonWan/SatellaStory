# People Directory

## Introduction

This directory catalogues every known individual who has been significantly involved with the Satellaview ecosystem -- from original Nintendo and St.GIGA staff to modern-day preservationists, researchers, translators, and fan community members.

Each entry includes:
- Known handles and real names (where verified)
- Role and contributions
- Contact information (where publicly available and verifiable)
- Source references for all claims

!!! warning "Privacy Note"
    Only contact information that has been **publicly posted** by the individual themselves is listed here. We do not include private or non-public contact details. If you are listed here and wish to update or remove your information, please contact us.
