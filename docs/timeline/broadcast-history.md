# Broadcast History

## The Satellaview Guide Era

Starting in 1997, St.GIGA shifted toward the **Satellaview Guide** format -- a service providing news, game tips, and magazine-style articles rather than full game downloads. This shift reflected declining investment in the platform and the difficulty of producing regular SoundLink content.[^1]

## The Final Broadcasts

The last regular Satellaview broadcasts occurred around **June 1999**. The SatellaOFF group's St.GIGA timeline shows Year 5 (April-May 1999) as consisting only of Satellaview Guide content before broadcasts ended entirely.[^2]

## Post-Service Period

After the service ended, the Satellaview community moved online. Key developments:

- **2000-2004:** Japanese fan sites proliferate (History Museum, Memorial, Mikarin)
- **2004:** First BS-X BIOS emulation becomes available (September 24)[^3]
- **2005:** BS analog broadcasting officially ends (March 31)[^4]
- **2006:** d4s releases first Satellaview ROM patch[^5]
- **2008:** English-language Satellablog founded[^6]
- **2010:** BS-X Project begins; first homebrew created[^7]
- **2011:** First successful BS-X SRAM dumps[^8]
- **2016:** BS-X English translation patch finalized[^9]
- **2017:** Satellaview emulation added to official SNES9X[^10]
- **2024-2026:** Ongoing ROM dump release cycle via Satellablog[^11]
