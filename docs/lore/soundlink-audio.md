# SoundLink & Audio

## What is SoundLink?

**SoundLink** was the Satellaview's signature feature: games that synchronized gameplay with live radio broadcasts. Players would tune into St.GIGA's satellite audio channel while playing, and the game would respond to the broadcast in real time.[^1]

## How SoundLink Worked

The SoundLink system required:

1. **Satellaview hardware** connected to the Super Famicom
2. **BS-X cartridge** with the Memory Pack inserted
3. **Satellite dish** tuned to BS-5 channel
4. **St.GIGA subscription** for the data broadcast service
5. **Radio receiver** (or TV with satellite tuner) for the audio portion

The game cartridge contained the base gameplay code, but key elements were triggered by the broadcast signal. During designated "SoundLink" periods, the game would:[^2]

- Receive timing signals from the satellite to synchronize with radio audio
- Display visual cues corresponding to radio narration
- Enable special events and content only during broadcast windows
- Track player performance for broadcast leaderboards

## Audio Content Types

### Live Narration
Radio personalities would narrate gameplay, provide backstory, and guide players through levels. This was especially prominent in:

- **BS Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets** -- full voice acting for characters
- **BS Fire Emblem: Akaneia Senki** -- radio drama segments between battles
- **BS Tantei Club: Yuki ni Kieru Kako** -- narrated mystery segments

### Music Broadcasts
St.GIGA broadcast music that was synchronized with gameplay. The music changed week-to-week in multi-episode games. Music restoration projects have attempted to recover these broadcasts from VHS recordings.[^3]

### Sound Effects
Some SoundLink games incorporated broadcast audio as dynamic sound effects -- enemy roars, environmental sounds, or event cues that were not present in the base ROM data.

## The Ephemeral Nature of SoundLink

SoundLink games were inherently **time-limited experiences**:
- They could only be played during designated broadcast windows
- Gameplay could not be saved (the game existed in RAM, not the Memory Pack)
- Rebroadcasts were rare and sometimes altered[^4]
- **Most SoundLink content is lost forever** because recordings were not officially preserved

## Audio Preservation

Fan preservation of SoundLink audio has taken several approaches:

1. **VHS recordings** -- Fans recorded broadcasts to VHS tape, capturing both the video output and audio. These tapes have been the primary source for audio restoration[^5]
2. **Audio splicing** -- Extracting "clean" portions of songs from VHS recordings and splicing them into contiguous wholes[^6]
3. **Album sources** -- Some Satellaview music was released on official soundtrack albums (e.g., BS Zelda music was available on Zelda soundtrack releases)[^7]
4. **Fan recreation** -- Using audio tools to recreate Satellaview music based on known compositions, notably via Nico Nico Douga (for BS Fire Emblem music)[^8]

## The St.GIGA Audio Experience

Beyond game audio, St.GIGA broadcast ambient music and personality segments. St.GIGA Forever documented the ambient music portions of St.GIGA's broadcasts, which included a distinctive soundscape that merged Japanese radio aesthetics with experimental electronic music.[^9]

## Where to Experience SoundLink Today

While the original broadcasts are long gone, several recordings survive online:

### BS Zelda Series
- **BS Zelda Map 1 — original broadcast gameplay** (YouTube, uploaded by makuchan): shows how a SoundLink download worked with live narration[^10]
- **BS Zelda Map 1 — annotated complete run** (YouTube, 4 parts): subtitled gameplay with preserved voice acting[^11]
- **BS Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets — week 4 voice acting translation** (BS Zelda Shrine): full transcript with video of the original broadcast narration[^12]
- **BS Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets — all weeks** (NicoVideo): complete broadcast recordings with voice acting, discovered 2007[^13]

### BS Fire Emblem: Akaneia Senki

All 4 episodes of the original broadcast survive on video:

- **Episode 1** — complete broadcast with radio drama segments and live commentary[^14]
- **Episode 2** — "Red Wind Ron", mirrored on Satellablog[^15]
- **Episodes 3 & 4** — complete finale, uploaded 2010 after the DS remake announcement[^16]

### Other SoundLink Games
- **Excitebike Bunbun Mario Battle Stadium — longplay** (YouTube, by DolmaKalem): 56-minute complete gameplay without SoundLink audio[^17]
- **Satellaview+** (satellaviewplus.github.io): live simulated St.GIGA broadcasts, currently active with emulation client — listen to recreated broadcasts in real time[^18]

### Audio Restoration Projects
- **KiddoCabbusses Bandcamp** (kiddocabbusses.bandcamp.com): restored Satellaview soundtrack releases[^19]
- **Satellaview Soundtracks** (Satellablog, 2009): documented music restoration from VHS recordings[^3]
- **BS Zelda music from album sources** (Satellablog, 2009): official soundtrack releases containing BS Zelda music[^7]
