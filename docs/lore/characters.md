# Characters of BS-X

## Introduction

The town of BS-X is populated by a cast of distinctive characters who provide services, information, and personality to the interface. The following information has been compiled from archived fan sites, the BS Zelda Shrine, the Satellaview History Museum's map, and the Japanese Satellaview Wiki.[^1]

!!! warning "Incomplete Data"
    Due to the loss of original source materials, some character names and details may be incomplete or reconstructed from multiple sources.

---

## The Player Avatar

The player character is the unnamed protagonist who arrives in the town. Their appearance is determined by the gender selected at configuration:[^2]

- **Male Avatar** -- Wears a backward red baseball cap, blue shirt, and blue pants
- **Female Avatar** -- Wears a pink dress/outfit with a pink ponytail

The avatar renders in a small chibi-style sprite that can walk through the town, interact with NPCs, and access services. The same avatar model appears in broadcast games including BS Zelda and BS Fire Emblem when launched from the BS-X cartridge.[^3]

---

## Key NPCs

### The Postman (Mail Delivery NPC)
Located near the town entrance. Delivers broadcast notifications and system messages. Described by the Satellaview History Museum as the first NPC the player encounters.[^4]

### The Old Man at the Download Center
Manages the game and data download functionality. Players speak to him to browse available broadcasts and initiate downloads.[^5]

### The Fortune Teller
Provides broadcast schedule information and sometimes cryptic hints about upcoming content. Located in a dedicated stall in the town square.[^6]

### The Bulletin Board Keeper
Maintains the "kabe shinbun" (wall newspaper) -- a broadcast newspaper with news, tips, and game information that updated regularly via satellite.[^7]

### Shopkeepers and Vendors
Various unnamed vendors around the town sell or provide access to different services:
- Memory Pack management services
- Event Plaza access
- Magazine downloads[^8]

## Seasonal and Time-Limited Characters

BS-X featured characters that only appeared during specific times or events:
- **Holiday characters** -- Appeared during seasonal broadcasts (Christmas, New Year, etc.)
- **Event-specific NPCs** -- Temporary characters that appeared during special promotions
- **The SatellaQuiz host** -- Appeared during quiz broadcast events[^9]

## The "Oshougatsu-san" NPC

The NPC named "oshougatsu-san" appeared during the SatellaQuiz segments of the SatellaOFF events, functioning as a quiz opponent with a score of 232/500 in the recorded session. This NPC's name translates approximately to "New Year's person," suggesting it may have been a seasonal character.[^10]

---

## Character Art

The only known original fan illustrations of the Satellaview community characters were drawn by **Blackeye** (webmaster of "BS-X no Hanashi") for the SatellaOFF event pages. These include portraits of the event attendees in a chibi style, which likely drew inspiration from the BS-X avatar design language.[^11] No official BS-X character art has been released by Nintendo.
