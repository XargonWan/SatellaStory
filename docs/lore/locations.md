# Locations in BS-X

## The Town

The BS-X town is a 2D top-down village divided into several districts. The player enters the town from **Ransei Bridge** (らんせい橋), which spans across a river at the town's entrance. The Satellaview History Museum is one of the few sources that documented the town's map layout.[^1]

## Key Locations

### Ransei Bridge (らんせい橋)
The starting point of the game. The bridge crosses a river at the town's entrance and serves as the primary gateway. This is where the player first arrives and begins their exploration.[^2]

### Town Square (中央広場)
The central hub of the town, connecting to all major districts. The town square features:
- A central fountain or monument
- Benches where NPCs occasionally sit
- Access to all directional paths[^3]

### Download Center (ダウンロードセンター)
Located off the town square, this building is where players browse and download broadcasted games and data. An elderly NPC manages the service and provides information about available downloads.[^4]

### Event Plaza (イベント広場)
A dedicated area for time-limited downloadable content. During active broadcasts, special events, competitions, and promotions were hosted here. The content was updated via satellite on a regular schedule.[^5]

### Memory Pack Station
A management interface for the Memory Pack, allowing players to view, delete, and organize saved content. Located in a dedicated building.[^6]

### Magazine Stand (雑誌スタンド)
Where players could access downloaded game magazines, "kabe shinbun" (wall newspaper) content, and news updates delivered via satellite.[^7]

### Fortune Teller's Stall
A small booth where a fortune teller NPC provides information about upcoming broadcast schedules and sometimes cryptic hints about future content.[^8]

## Residential Areas

The town includes residential districts with houses that NPCs inhabit. Some buildings are purely decorative; others serve minor functions or contain NPCs who provide flavor dialogue. The town's layout suggests it was designed to feel lived-in despite its primarily functional purpose as an operating system.[^9]

## Seasonal Decoration

The town would change its appearance based on:
- **Seasonal events** -- decorations for holidays (Christmas, New Year, etc.)
- **Special broadcasts** -- banners or signs announcing major content drops
- **Time of day** -- lighting and NPC behavior changes[^10]

## The Name-Theft Mystery

Throughout the town, signs and banners with the town's name are conspicuously blank or obscured. NPCs comment on this, referencing the central premise: the town's name was stolen. The player's exploration and participation in broadcasts gradually uncover the story behind the theft, though how much of this narrative was planned versus emergent from the service's structure is unclear.[^11]

## Unknown Locations

Due to the loss of original documentation and the time-limited nature of some events, not all locations in the BS-X town have been documented. Some areas may have been added or modified during the service's lifetime via satellite updates and are now lost.[^12]
