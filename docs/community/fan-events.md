# Fan Events

## SatellaOFF (1999)

The first known real-life meetups of Satellaview enthusiasts. See [Historical Communities](../community/historical-communities.md) for full details.

**Event 1:** March 28-29, 1999 -- Higashi-Shinagawa, Tokyo (6 participants)
**Event 2:** April 29, 1999 -- Kameido/Nakano, Tokyo (3 participants)[^1]

## BS Zelda Master Quest (2009)

On April 11, 2009, the BS Zelda Shrine held a timed competition through a modified hard version of Ancient Stone Tablets:

- **Format:** Players played all 4 weeks of the Master Quest (harder dungeons, harder bosses)
- **Scoring:** Followed original April 1997 broadcast rules (deaths: -10,000 pts, rupees: +10 pts)
- **Winner:** Phoenix (phoenix20xx@gmail.com) -- entry in Hall of Fame
- **Deadline:** Extended from April 19 to May 4, 2009 due to low initial participation[^2]

## Satellaview+ Live Revival

The Satellaview+ project (satellaviewplus.github.io) provides ongoing simulated St.GIGA broadcasts, functioning as a continuous "event" for the community. Users can connect emulators or real hardware to receive broadcasts as if during the original service period.[^3]
